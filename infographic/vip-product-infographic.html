How the product works

One selected company moves through the VIP value engine

This infographic uses an illustrative manufacturing SME profile, but the stages and scoring logic match the real product.

VIP does not start with a blank form. It starts with a selected company and an existing operating footprint.
Quantitative evidence is pulled automatically. The entrepreneur adds the strategic context data cannot see on its own.
The company is always judged in relative terms, not in isolation. Position inside a peer set is what makes the score meaningful.
The output is not only a number. It is a value picture, a risk read, a capital profile, and a decision queue.

Benchmarking logic

The company is judged against similar businesses before any capital score is assembled.

This is what makes VIP more than an internal scorecard. It gives the entrepreneur a relative position, not just an isolated answer.

1

Closest peer group first

VIP first queries the company’s attached peer group so the first benchmark is as close as possible to the chosen business model and sector profile.

2

Broader NACE cohort if needed

If the close group is too thin, the lens widens to the NACE prefix. This protects the percentile from being based on too few comparables.

3

Calibrated priors where the market is silent

Some variables, such as recurring revenue or concentration resilience, do not exist directly inside the market data. VIP uses calibrated priors so those signals still rank consistently.
